- cons: delays the next vertical

## 12. Option B Review

- purpose: start the next student lifecycle vertical around admissions, students, enrollment, records, transcripts, appeals, and interventions
- pros: expands product breadth and moves closer to a fuller University OS
- cons: risks diluting the newly completed executive governance stack before consolidation
- assessment: viable later, but premature as the immediate next move

## 13. Option C Review

- purpose: start an HR / staff governance vertical
- pros: connects to university operations and could appeal to executive leadership
- cons: introduces sensitive HR and punitive-scoring risk surfaces before the current suite is unified
- assessment: lower priority than consolidating the existing suite

## 14. Option D Review

- cons: requires careful evidence and compliance contracts that are better supported after suite consolidation
- assessment: attractive follow-on path, but not the recommended immediate next step
