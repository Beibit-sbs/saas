# A-056.5 Runtime - Digital Twin Capacity Early-Warning Report

## Scope

The twin starts WARNING: turn the capacity projection into severity-classified
early warnings with a recommended human action — read-only, human-gated, no
autonomy, no fabricated signal data.

## Result

- Backend route: `POST /api/admin/digital-twin/early-warning/capacity` (reuses
  `digital_twin.simulate.run`; route count 3→4, permission unchanged at 3).
- Service `capacity_early_warning`: runs the capacity what-if (live enrollments
  via A-056.4) and classifies each capacity ratio:
  - `> 1.2` → `high`, `> 1.0` → `medium`, else no warning.
  - classroom risk → `escalate_to_scheduling_and_facilities`; dormitory risk →
    `escalate_to_housing_office`.
- Each warning carries `requires_human_approval=true`, `no_autonomous_action=true`.
- `candidate_signal_sources` declares the 5 signal registries the warning WOULD
  consume once live-instance wiring exists — declared, not fabricated.
- Frontend: read-only "Early warning" section on /console/digital-twin.

## Honesty / Safety

- No fabricated signal counts: the student_risk/finance_anomaly/etc. registries
  are governance/contract modules, so they are listed as candidate sources to be
  live-wired, NOT read as fake numbers.
- The twin proposes; a human decides. It recommends an action and executes nothing.
- `fake_metrics=false`, `human_review_required=true`; viewer denied (403).
