## 9. Selected Vertical Product Definition

Campus / Facilities / Housing / Transport Suite is defined as a university campus operations
product layer for campus assets, buildings, rooms, facilities, dormitory/housing visibility,
maintenance/service request metadata, transport coordination metadata, occupancy/availability
visibility, safety readiness visibility, and rectorate-level campus dashboard evidence surfaces.

## 10. Selected Vertical Scope

1. Campus overview
2. Facilities registry

4. Room visibility

6. Maintenance request metadata
7. Service request tracking metadata
8. Transport coordination metadata
9. Occupancy / availability visibility
10. Facility ownership / responsible unit metadata
11. Access / visitor bridge
12. Safety / compliance readiness bridge
13. Student services bridge
14. Finance / asset bridge
15. HR / staff assignment bridge
16. Rectorate campus dashboard
17. Audit / evidence / limitation model

## 11. Selected Vertical Non-Scope / Forbidden Claims

Forbidden claims and implementations:
- no fake IoT/live sensor integration
- no fake GPS/live transport tracking
- no fake building automation
- no fake access-control enforcement
- no fake safety certification
- no fake maintenance completion
- no fake official occupancy guarantee
- no automatic eviction/housing decision
- no automatic student/staff sanction
- no autonomous dispatch
- no external provider/live integration unless evidenced
- no production-ready/sales-ready/GCC-ready/L5/L6 claims

Allowed behavior in bounded scope:
- metadata/readiness/evidence visibility
- human-reviewed service request tracking
- dashboard summaries with incomplete-data flags
- tenant/RBAC-guarded views
- audit evidence and limitation disclosure
