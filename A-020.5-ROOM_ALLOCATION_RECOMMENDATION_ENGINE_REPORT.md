## 4) Contracts and Logic Delivered

In `backend/app/modules/scheduling/room_allocation_readiness.py`:

- `RoomAllocationRecommendationStatus`
- `RoomAllocationCandidate`
- `RoomAllocationRecommendationEvidence`
- `RoomAllocationRecommendationResult`
- `build_room_allocation_recommendation_result()`

Deterministic ranking order:

1. Higher `recommendation_score`
2. Lower `risk_level`
3. Fewer conflicts
4. Fewer mismatch reasons
5. Smaller non-negative capacity delta
6. `room_id` ascending (stable deterministic tie-break)

Safety behaviors:

- Reject tenant mismatch across candidate capability/conflict evidence
- Recommendation-only output (no assignment/reservation ids)
- No schedule/booking mutation side effects
