## 1. Executive Summary

A-037.0-SPEC selects the next major product vertical after the closure of Executive Governance Suite, Student Lifecycle Suite, and Academic Operations Suite. This action is spec-only, docs-only, and product-strategy only. No backend runtime, frontend runtime, migrations, tests, Playwright specs, seeded data, screenshots, demo evidence, real research data, or external integrations were added or changed in this action.

The selected fourth product vertical is Research / Science Suite. It is the most natural next University OS layer because universities are not only teaching institutions. A credible University OS must also cover student research work, scientific supervision, publications, conferences, grants, ethics approval workflows, research evidence, and research-facing dashboards.

## 5. Why Research / Science Suite Now

- it completes the university mission beyond governance, student lifecycle, and academic operations
- it covers scientific productivity, research accountability, and evidence lineage
- it gives rectorate and management a research-facing visibility layer for publications, grants, conferences, projects, and ethics queues
- it prepares future accreditation and rankings evidence without claiming official scoring or external verification
- it connects naturally to Executive Governance, Academic Operations, Student Lifecycle, Library / Knowledge / Archive, and future Quality / Accreditation work
- it creates a clean future lane for research Brain signals such as grant execution risk, publication evidence quality, supervision delay, and research output gaps
