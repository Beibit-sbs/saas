## 6. Recommended Vertical

Recommended vertical: `Student Lifecycle Suite`

Reason:

- Executive Governance Suite closes the top-management governance layer
- Student Lifecycle Suite closes the core student operating layer
- together they move SBS UB materially closer to a full University OS rather than a leadership-only governance system
- this vertical also creates the cleanest bridge between governance, academic processes, and student success without claiming production readiness

## 7. Student Lifecycle Suite Identity

Suite name:

- `Student Lifecycle Suite`

Internal tagline:

- `From applicant to student record, enrollment, academic progress, intervention, and graduation readiness.`

Internal description:

- Student Lifecycle Suite is the student operating layer of SBS UB. It connects applicant intake, admissions, enrollment, student profile, academic records, degree progress, requests and appeals, interventions, and graduation readiness into one governed student lifecycle.

Not this:

- not an LMS
- not a fake student database
- not an autonomous admission decision engine
- not a fake transcript generator
- not a production SIS replacement yet
- not a sales-ready claim

This is:

- product vertical selection
- spec and planning stage
- next vertical after Executive Governance Suite
- future suite for student operations
