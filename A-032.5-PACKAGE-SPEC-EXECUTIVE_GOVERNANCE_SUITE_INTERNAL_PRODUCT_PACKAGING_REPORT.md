## 10. Internal Demo Story

- demo_title: From Rector Assignment to Document Execution and Audit

Seven-minute internal demo flow:
1. Rector creates assignment.
2. Executor receives and accepts.
3. Assignment links to official document or decree.
4. Document moves through review.
5. Decree records signed metadata only.
6. Incoming correspondence is routed.
7. Executor submits report and evidence.
8. SLA and overdue state appears.
9. Dashboard updates.
10. Audit trail and archive preserve evidence.

Demo constraints:
- real implemented features only
- no fake provider delivery
- no fake signature
- no fake registry
- no AI autonomous decision
