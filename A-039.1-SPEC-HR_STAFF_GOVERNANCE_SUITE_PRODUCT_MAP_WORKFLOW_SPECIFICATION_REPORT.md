## 5. Product Vertical Identity

Product vertical: `HR / Staff Governance Suite`

Product goal:

Provide a metadata/evidence-first staff lifecycle, HR governance, workload bridge, access lifecycle, staff compliance, and HR operations visibility foundation for university leadership and administrative teams.

Primary users:

- rector / executive leadership
- vice rector
- HR director
- HR specialists
- dean / faculty leadership
- department heads
- academic operations staff
- finance / payroll observer
- IAM / security admin
- legal / compliance reviewer
- internal auditor
- staff member / employee self-service user
- human review committee

Operating mode:

- `METADATA_EVIDENCE_HUMAN_REVIEW_ONLY`
- no automatic employment decision
- no payroll execution
- no provider live integration
- no disciplinary automation
- no hidden score

This is:

- a bounded future product vertical for HR governance and staff lifecycle visibility
- a bridge-first evidence layer over payroll, workload, IAM, document, and audit canonicals
- a foundation for future backend/frontend/E2E contract work

This is not:

- not a payroll system replacement
- not an IAM system replacement
- not an autonomous HR decision engine
- not a hidden employee ranking system
- not a live 1C connector
- not a production-ready or GCC-ready suite
