# Auditor Guide

## Purpose

Guide internal auditors through traceability, evidence review, and archive checks in the pilot package.

## Intended Users

- Internal Auditor

## What The Role Can Do

- review audit trail
- review status history
- review evidence completeness
- review archive integrity

## What The Role Cannot Do

- no operational mutation
- no hard delete
- no fake KPI normalization
- no autonomous decision

## Basic Workflow

1. Open audit trail or history view.
2. Review linked evidence.
3. Confirm archive state and traceability.
4. Record gaps for pilot review.

## Limitations

- no production-ready claim
- no provider integration
- no real personal data
- no L5/L6 claim

## Support Notes

- classify missing traceability as pilot readiness gap
- do not restate incomplete metrics as facts

## Pilot Feedback Questions

- Was audit navigation clear?
- Were evidence expectations explicit?
- What traceability gaps remain?
