# Chancellery / Document Controller Guide

## Purpose

Provide a pilot-oriented guide for official document, decree, correspondence, and archive handling.

## Intended Users

- Chancellery Clerk
- Document Controller
- Legal Reviewer for adjacent lifecycle review

## What The Role Can Do

- manage document workflow
- manage decree lifecycle steps
- route correspondence
- review archive state
- interpret metadata-only labels

## What The Role Cannot Do

- no fake signature
- no fake sent/delivered claim
- no provider integration
- no hard delete

## Basic Workflow

1. Open the relevant governance record.
2. Review lifecycle state.
3. Maintain routing and metadata quality.
4. Confirm archive readiness when complete.

## Limitations

- signed metadata remains metadata-only
- sent/delivered remains metadata-only
- no sales-ready claim
- no real personal data

## Support Notes

- log missing lifecycle handoffs as pilot issues
- keep all registry and delivery language honest

## Pilot Feedback Questions

- Are lifecycle labels clear enough?
- Are metadata-only labels explicit enough?
- Which routing steps need better guidance?
