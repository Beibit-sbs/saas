# Demo Scenario

## 1. Demo Objective

Demonstrate the Executive Governance Suite as an internal governance operating layer from rector decision through execution control, evidence, SLA, audit, archive, and executive visibility.

## 2. Pre-Demo Setup

- confirm the environment uses placeholder-only demo context
- confirm no real personal data is displayed
- confirm Control Tower metrics show honest incomplete, foundation, or future labels where applicable
- confirm linked assignment and governance workflow routes are reachable
- confirm limitations are prepared verbally and on-screen where available

## 3. Actor Roles

- Rector / President
- Chief of Staff / Executive Secretary
- Executor / Responsible Officer
- Chancellery Clerk or Document Controller
- Internal Auditor

## 4. Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. Open Executive Control Tower.
2. Show incomplete and foundation metric labels honestly.
3. Open Rector Assignment OS.
4. Show assignment lifecycle state and linked governance context.
5. Show report and evidence flow.
6. Open Document / Decree / Correspondence Workflow OS.
7. Show linked governance workflow progression.
8. Show SLA, overdue, and manual-confirmation escalation visibility.
9. Show audit trail, status history, and archive visibility.
10. Return to Control Tower.
11. Close with limitations and next hardening steps.

## 5. Expected Visible Proof

- Control Tower read-only visibility
- honest incomplete/foundation/future labels
- assignment lifecycle surfaces
- report and evidence surfaces
- linked document, decree, or correspondence surfaces
- SLA and overdue visibility
- audit/history/archive visibility

## 6. Limitations To Say During Demo

- no production-ready claim
- no sales-ready claim
- no live provider integration
- no fake KPI
- no autonomous decision
- suite-level E2E is not yet implemented as one unified live path

## 7. Do-Not-Claim Section

- do not claim production-ready
- do not claim sales-ready
- do not claim auto-signature
- do not claim live provider dispatch
- do not claim fake KPI
- do not claim autonomous decision

## 8. Fallback Plan If Route Or Data Is Unavailable

- switch to the nearest already-evidenced vertical route
- explain the missing cross-suite connection as future unified demo path
- keep all labels honest and avoid fake pilot evidence
- use screenshots or route references only if already captured in existing evidence
