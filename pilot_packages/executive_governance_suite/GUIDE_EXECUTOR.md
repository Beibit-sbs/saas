# Executor Guide

## Purpose

Help executors understand assignment acceptance, report submission, and evidence attachment in the pilot context.

## Intended Users

- Executor / Responsible Officer
- Department Director / Dean for owned-flow review

## What The Role Can Do

- accept assignment
- submit report
- attach evidence
- respond to revision

## What The Role Cannot Do

- no fake evidence
- no approval bypass
- no hard delete
- no credentials sharing

## Basic Workflow

1. Open assignment detail.
2. Review linked governance context.
3. Accept assignment.
4. Submit report and evidence.
5. Respond to revision if returned.

## Limitations

- no real personal data
- no provider integration
- no production-ready claim
- no autonomous decision

## Support Notes

- report missing status transitions through pilot triage
- use placeholders only in examples or screenshots

## Pilot Feedback Questions

- Was the report flow understandable?
- Was evidence upload guidance clear?
- Which status transitions were confusing?
