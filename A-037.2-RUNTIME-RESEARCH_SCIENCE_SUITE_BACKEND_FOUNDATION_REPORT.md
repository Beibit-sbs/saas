# A-037.2-RUNTIME - Research / Science Suite Backend Foundation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

A-037.2-RUNTIME implements the controlled Research / Science backend foundation defined by A-037.2-SPEC and grounded in the A-037.1-SPEC product map. The delivered slice is backend-only, metadata-and-evidence-first, tenant fail-closed, RBAC-protected, audit-safe, and explicitly bounded against fake outputs or overclaims.

The runtime foundation adds a new `research_science` backend module, a bounded `rs_` table family, dashboard/audit/evidence/bridge support, strict tenant enforcement, route registration, RBAC exposure, an Alembic migration, and focused backend tests. It does not enable provider execution, external database synchronization, official verification, hidden researcher scoring, fake publications, fake grant evidence, fake conference certificates, autonomous ethics approval, autonomous grant submission, or autonomous publication verification.
