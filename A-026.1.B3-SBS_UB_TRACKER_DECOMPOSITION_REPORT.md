## 3. Problem Statement
SBS_UB.md had grown into a mixed document containing control state, historical waves, inventory, evidence, and roadmap. This increased navigation and maintenance risk and created source-of-truth ambiguity.

## 4. Decomposition Strategy

- Preserve facts/metrics/history by explicit carry-over and authoritative references.
- Keep baseline and extension metrics separated.
