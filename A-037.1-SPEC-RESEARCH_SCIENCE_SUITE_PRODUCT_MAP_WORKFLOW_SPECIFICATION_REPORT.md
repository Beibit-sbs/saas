## 4. Product Identity

Product vertical: `Research / Science Suite`

Tagline:

From student research work and scientific supervision to publications, conferences, grants, ethics approval, research evidence, and science dashboards.

Positioning:

Research / Science Suite is the fourth SBS UB product vertical after Executive Governance, Student Lifecycle, and Academic Operations. It covers the university research mission and provides controlled internal visibility into research activity, scientific outputs, grants, conferences, ethics and compliance, evidence, and research dashboards.

Product scope:

- internal research operations baseline
- metadata and evidence first research governance
- dashboard visibility with explicit incomplete-data boundaries
- bridge-first reuse across executive, academic, student, library, document, finance, and future provider layers

This is not:

- not an official publication verification engine
- not an official grant submission engine
- not an official ethics committee automation engine
- not a hidden researcher ranking system
- not an autonomous research scoring engine
- not a production research repository replacement
- not a sales-ready or GCC-ready suite
- not an L5/L6 claim

This is:

- the planning foundation for Research / Science backend and frontend contract definition
- a controlled research governance and evidence visibility layer
- a University OS research mission slice with human review required
