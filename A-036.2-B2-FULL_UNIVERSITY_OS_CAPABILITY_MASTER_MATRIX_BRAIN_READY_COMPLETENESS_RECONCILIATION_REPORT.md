## 4. Why This Matrix Is Needed

`A-036.2-B1.R1` proved a central planning risk: a desired capability list can mix existing canonicals, adjacent canonicals, bridges, and true-new modules. Without a single authoritative matrix, future work risks:

- forgetting major University OS capabilities beyond the baseline `150`
- duplicating already-existing canonical modules under new vertical names
- treating workflows, dashboards, integrations, Brain signals, and bridges as if they were all new backend packages
- narrowing SBS UB to an artificially small platform ambition

The matrix solves that by keeping the entire platform capability universe visible in one place.
