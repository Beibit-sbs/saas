Page-specific boundary labels:

- Standards: `Standards mapping is internal readiness metadata only. It does not approve accreditation or confirm official compliance.`
- Evidence: `Evidence records are metadata-only unless reviewed by humans. No external/provider verification or official submission is claimed.`
- Program readiness: `Program readiness is an internal review signal, not an official accreditation decision or hidden program score.`
- Institutional readiness: `Institutional readiness is evidence-backed metadata for internal review. No official ministry submission is performed.`
- Self-assessment: `Self-assessment reports are draft/internal review artifacts only. No official submission is performed.`
- Improvement plans: `Improvement plans track human-owned actions. No autonomous sanction or automatic program closure is performed.`
- Internal audits: `Internal quality audit records are human-review artifacts. No automatic penalty or hidden score is created.`
- Stakeholder feedback: `Feedback metadata does not create fake survey results or hidden student/faculty/program scores.`
- Committee: `Committee workflow records human decisions only. No automatic accreditation approval is performed.`
- External review: `External review metadata does not claim fake expert approval or official external verification.`
- Gap analysis: `Gap analysis is deterministic metadata for human review. It is not a hidden quality score.`
- Bridges: `Read-only-first bridge. No cross-suite mutation or provider sync is allowed by default.`
- Limitations: `Frontend runtime is not production-ready. Provider integrations, official approval, ministry submission, ranking claims, and L5/L6 readiness are not implemented.`
