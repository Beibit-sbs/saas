## Architecture

### Health Model (deterministic, no LLM)

5 dimensions with weighted scoring:

| Dimension | Weight | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| budget_health | 0.30 | budget_planning/service.py |
| procurement_health | 0.25 | procurement/service.py |
| po_delivery_health | 0.20 | procurement/service.py |
| asset_conversion_health | 0.15 | asset_inventory/service.py |
| risk_signal_health | 0.10 | brain_core decisions count |

**Score → Status:** 80+ healthy, 60+ watch, 40+ risk, <40 critical  
**Score → Risk level:** <40 critical, <60 high, <75 medium, else low

### Brain Core Wiring

- Signal types: `finance.operations.health_check`, `finance.operations.risk_detected`
- Signal class: `financial_risk`, scenario: `finance_operations_health`
- 4 classifier paths: `_critical`, `_high`, `_medium`, `_low`
- 4 rules-engine rules mapping to `decision_type=finance_health`
- Actions: `create_finance_health_review_task`, `notify_finance`, `notify_procurement_team`
