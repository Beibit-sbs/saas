## 5. Sensitive-Domain Risk Classification

### Risk Level: HIGH

Academic integrity case management carries the highest risk classification in the student lifecycle because:

1. **Finding risk**: Any automated output could be interpreted as an academic integrity finding (plagiarism, cheating, unauthorized collaboration)
2. **Grade impact risk**: Academic integrity findings typically trigger grade penalties (zero on assignment, course failure, grade amendment)
3. **Disciplinary cascade risk**: Academic integrity findings escalate to formal disciplinary procedures
4. **Student status risk**: Findings can lead to suspension, dismissal, academic probation
5. **Reputation risk**: Findings are permanent records that affect transcripts, references, professional licensing
6. **Systemic bias risk**: Automated integrity assessment systems have documented bias against non-native speakers, certain writing styles, cultural communication patterns
7. **Due process risk**: Automated determination deprives students of procedural fairness rights
8. **Legal liability risk**: Wrongful academic integrity findings expose institutions to legal action

### Risk Mitigation Model
- READINESS_AND_EVIDENCE_ONLY: no decision output
- Human review gate required at every stage transition
- Committee review required before any action
- Fairness review required before committee decision
- Appeal boundary metadata required for all case records
- No automated outcome of any kind

| `recommendation` | Recommendations imply directional AI bias toward outcome |

| `external_submission` | No data leaves the system without human authorization |
| `hidden_score` | No scoring of any kind (integrity score, suspicion score, etc.) |
| `synthetic_score` | No generated/synthetic scoring |
| `discriminatory_score` | No scoring that could introduce bias |
| `llm_call` | No LLM integration (AI judgement forbidden) |
