## 4. Suite Identity

- suite name: Executive Governance Suite
- internal product tagline: From rector decision to execution, evidence, SLA, audit, archive, and executive visibility.
- internal description: Executive Governance Suite is a governance operating layer for university leadership. It connects executive assignments, official documents, decrees, correspondence, SLA, escalation, evidence, audit, archive, and control tower visibility into one internal product suite.
- unified product story: a rector or executive decision begins a governed chain of assignment, document handling, execution follow-up, evidence submission, SLA visibility, traceable audit history, archival preservation, and read-only executive oversight.
- this is: internal product suite; executive governance layer; evidence-backed workflow system; read-only executive visibility layer; pilot-package candidate after additional hardening
- this is not: task tracker; document-only product; dashboard-only product; LMS; ERP replacement; production-ready claim; sales-ready claim

## 5. Unified Core Flow

1. Rector or executive decision is created.
2. Assignment is issued to an executor or department.
3. Assignment can be linked to an official document, decree, resolution, or correspondence.
4. Executor accepts and works on the assignment.
5. Document, decree, or correspondence moves through its lifecycle.
6. Executor submits report and evidence.
7. SLA and overdue status are tracked.
8. Escalation policy is visible and remains manual-confirmation only.
9. Control Tower summarizes execution and risk indicators.
10. Audit trail and status history preserve traceability.
11. Archive preserves final state.

- explicit constraints: no auto-signature; no auto-approval; no autonomous decision; no auto-escalation; no provider delivery; no fake KPI; no fake registry; no fake sent/delivered; no hard delete; no production-ready claim
