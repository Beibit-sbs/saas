## 5. Next Vertical Options

Option A - Academic Operations Suite

- scope: curriculum, course catalog, academic calendar, timetable / schedule, attendance, teaching load, room / classroom allocation, exams, gradebook, academic operations dashboard
- strategic value: completes the academic operating layer, connects naturally to Student Lifecycle and Executive Governance visibility, strengthens demo value, and moves SBS UB closer to a complete University OS baseline

- scope: staff records, onboarding, leave, teaching load contracts, staff probation, performance review, exit/offboarding
- strategic value: strong internal administration value and staff-management support
- risks: HR sensitive data, scoring/discrimination risk, performance-review sensitivity

Option D - Finance / Procurement / Asset Suite

- scope: procurement, budget requests, contracts, assets, vendor management, finance approval workflows
- strategic value: financial and administrative relevance, management value
- risks: procurement compliance sensitivity, financial control sensitivity, legal and audit risk, and no automated procurement decisions

## 6. Selected Vertical

Selected vertical: Academic Operations Suite.

Reason: SBS UB already has governance and student lifecycle verticals closed. Academic Operations Suite is the best next strategic layer because it closes the daily academic operating system and makes the platform feel like an end-to-end University OS rather than a set of adjacent subsystems.

## 7. Academic Operations Suite Identity

Working tagline:

From curriculum and timetable to attendance, teaching load, exams, grades, and academic operations visibility.

Internal description:

Academic Operations Suite is the operational academic layer of SBS UB. It connects curriculum, course catalog, academic calendar, timetable, classroom and room allocation, teaching load, attendance, exam planning, gradebook metadata, and academic operations visibility into a governed academic workflow.

This is not:

- not an LMS
- not a fake gradebook
- not an automated grading system
- not an automated exam decision system
- not a production SIS replacement
- not a Platonus integration
- not a fake academic record source
- not production-ready
- not sales-ready
- not L5/L6

This is:

- the third major product vertical after Executive Governance Suite and Student Lifecycle Suite
- a strategy and selection specification
- the planning foundation for Academic Operations Suite product-map and workflow specification

## 9. Core Product Flows

Flow 1 - Curriculum to Course Catalog

- states: curriculum draft, curriculum reviewed, curriculum approved metadata, course catalog draft, course catalog published metadata, academic term linked
- boundaries: no fake official curriculum approval, metadata only until real governance source exists, human review required

Flow 2 - Academic Calendar to Timetable

- states: academic term configured, calendar draft, calendar reviewed, timetable draft, room/resource allocation pending, timetable published metadata, conflict review required
- boundaries: no automatic final scheduling without human review, no fake room availability, no fake teacher workload approval

Flow 3 - Teaching Load and Faculty Assignment

- states: teaching load draft, department review, faculty assignment metadata, workload conflict flagged, human review required, approved metadata
- boundaries: no automatic workload decision, no hidden faculty ranking, no performance score, no HR decision automation

Flow 4 - Attendance Operations

- states: attendance session planned, attendance collection pending, attendance metadata recorded, exception review required, attendance summary available, audit archived
- boundaries: no punitive automation, no automatic student sanction, no fake attendance data, no biometric/provider claim

Flow 5 - Exam Planning and Gradebook Metadata

- states: exam plan draft, exam schedule review, exam room allocation metadata, gradebook metadata opened, grade entry metadata recorded, grade review required, grade publication metadata
- boundaries: no fake grades, no automated grading, no official grade publication claim, no exam outcome automation, human review required

Flow 6 - Academic Operations Visibility

- states: dashboard computed from available metadata, incomplete data flagged, conflicts highlighted, human review queue visible, audit evidence available
- boundaries: fake_metrics=false, no invented academic KPI, no hidden score, no autonomous decisions

## 10. Role Model

1. Vice Rector for Academic Affairs
   - responsibilities: academic oversight, escalation review, cross-faculty decision visibility
   - suite areas: dashboard, calendar, timetable, exams, policy exceptions
   - allowed actions: review metadata, approve bounded review states, inspect conflicts and audit evidence
   - forbidden actions: automatic grading, hidden scoring, fake publication, automatic sanctions
   - sensitive data boundaries: aggregate visibility only where detailed source is incomplete

2. Registrar
   - responsibilities: term, catalog, gradebook metadata governance, publication review
   - suite areas: curriculum, catalog, calendar, gradebook metadata
   - allowed actions: manage reviewed metadata, open review queues, publish bounded metadata states
   - forbidden actions: fake grade publication, hidden score assignment, automated academic decisions
   - sensitive data boundaries: metadata-only posture where official source is incomplete

3. Department Director / Dean
   - responsibilities: department-level review for teaching load, timetable, and exam planning
   - suite areas: teaching load, timetable, exams, faculty assignment visibility
   - allowed actions: review assignments, flag conflicts, approve metadata states with audit trail
   - forbidden actions: hidden faculty ranking, automatic workload decisions, discriminatory scoring
   - sensitive data boundaries: no HR scoring or employment decisions

4. Program Chair
   - responsibilities: curriculum and course offering alignment, schedule review
   - suite areas: curriculum, catalog, timetable, calendar
   - allowed actions: draft and review academic structure metadata, raise exceptions
   - forbidden actions: fake official approval, automatic final scheduling
   - sensitive data boundaries: explicit incomplete-data warnings required

5. Faculty Member
   - responsibilities: assignment visibility, attendance metadata entry, gradebook metadata participation
   - suite areas: attendance, teaching load visibility, gradebook metadata, exam schedule visibility
   - allowed actions: submit metadata updates within authorized scope, view assigned schedule context
   - forbidden actions: automated grading actions, fake attendance, autonomous student sanctions
   - sensitive data boundaries: no hidden-score access, no sanction automation

6. Timetable Coordinator
   - responsibilities: timetable draft coordination and conflict management
   - suite areas: timetable, room allocation, calendar
   - allowed actions: draft schedules, flag conflicts, prepare review states
   - forbidden actions: automatic final scheduling, fake room availability claims
   - sensitive data boundaries: no hidden prioritization scores

7. Exam Office Coordinator
   - responsibilities: exam schedule review and room allocation metadata
   - suite areas: exam planning, room allocation, calendar
   - allowed actions: manage exam drafts, assign metadata, escalate conflicts
   - forbidden actions: exam outcome automation, fake exam completion claims
   - sensitive data boundaries: no grade/outcome fabrication

8. Academic Quality Officer
   - responsibilities: review exception patterns, quality visibility, policy conformance
   - suite areas: dashboard, policy exceptions, curriculum and catalog review context
   - allowed actions: inspect evidence, flag inconsistencies, review completeness gaps
   - forbidden actions: invented KPI, hidden quality score, automatic sanctions
   - sensitive data boundaries: limitations must remain explicit

9. Student Affairs Officer
   - responsibilities: cross-link operational issues that affect student-facing academic delivery
   - suite areas: attendance visibility, exceptions, dashboard summaries
   - allowed actions: view bounded metadata, raise review requests, monitor queues
   - forbidden actions: automatic student sanctions, hidden risk scoring
   - sensitive data boundaries: no punitive automation

10. Internal Auditor
   - responsibilities: audit evidence, traceability, review of metadata decisions and exceptions
   - suite areas: audit evidence across all modules
   - allowed actions: inspect audit records, review transition history, export internal evidence
   - forbidden actions: altering operational states without process authority
   - sensitive data boundaries: read-only audit posture

11. Platform Admin
   - responsibilities: platform configuration, access control, technical oversight
   - suite areas: all modules for admin configuration and permissions
   - allowed actions: configure access, manage platform settings, inspect technical health
   - forbidden actions: business-outcome fabrication, hidden scoring, autonomous academic decisions
   - sensitive data boundaries: administrative access does not imply authority to invent academic outcomes
