## 8. Role Model Preview

- Student Affairs Officer: intake, triage, and case coordination
- Student Support Specialist: case handling and follow-up planning
- Academic Advisor: advising context and intervention coordination
- Financial Aid Officer: aid-context review and dependency signals
- Disability/Accessibility Support Coordinator: accommodations support context and evidence checks
- Registrar: institutional review boundary and escalations
- Internal Auditor: evidence, audit, and boundary conformance
- Compliance/Legal Reviewer: sensitive-case policy and fairness boundary checks
- Platform Admin: tenant-safe operations and support tooling

All outcome decisions remain human-reviewed.
