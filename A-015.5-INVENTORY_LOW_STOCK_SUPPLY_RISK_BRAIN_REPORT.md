## Risk Classification Rules

| Condition | Risk Level | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| `qty <= 0` or `None` inputs | `critical` | `create_procurement_request`, `vendor_followup`*, `budget_review`* |
| `qty < threshold * 0.5` | `high` | `create_procurement_request`, `vendor_followup`* |
| `qty < threshold` | `medium` | `reorder_review`, `budget_review`* |
| `qty >= threshold` | `low` | (none) |

`*` conditional on vendor_id / budget_id being provided  

Missing `reorder_threshold` → `medium` (fail-safe, unknown state)
