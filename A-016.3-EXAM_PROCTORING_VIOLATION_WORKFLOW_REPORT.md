## 5. Deterministic Severity Model

| Level | Conditions |
|-------|-----------|
| **CRITICAL** | `manual_proctor_report=True` OR `risk_level=="critical"` OR face_mismatch + forbidden_app together OR `flag_count >= 4` OR (`confidence >= 0.95` AND severe violation type) |
| **HIGH** | `risk_level=="high"` OR forbidden_app event OR face_mismatch event OR multiple_faces event OR `flag_count >= 3` OR (`confidence >= 0.80` AND severe type) |
| **MEDIUM** | `risk_level=="medium"` OR camera_absent event OR suspicious_activity event OR `flag_count >= 1` OR `confidence >= 0.50` |
| **LOW** | Default (no flags, no significant confidence) |
