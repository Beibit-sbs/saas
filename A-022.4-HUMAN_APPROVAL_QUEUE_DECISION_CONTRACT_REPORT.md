## 7. Decision Semantics Proof
Implemented semantics:
- `approved/rejected/revision_requested` require `reviewer_id`
- `rejected/revision_requested` require `reviewer_note`
- `high/critical` decisions require explicit `reviewer_note`
- Terminal queue items cannot be decided again
- `approved` means decision recorded only
- Optional explicit proposal FSM update supported (`apply_proposal_fsm=True`) and still non-destructive
