### 9.2 Readiness Status Values (Decision Tree)

| Status | Trigger | Meaning | Next Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **READY_FOR_REVIEW** | evidence_completeness ≥ 90 AND no blocked dependencies | All required evidence present; ready for human review | Route to human decision-maker |
| **PARTIAL_EVIDENCE** | 60 ≤ evidence_completeness < 90 | Most evidence present; some gaps | Request missing evidence pieces |
| **INCOMPLETE_EVIDENCE** | 30 ≤ evidence_completeness < 60 | Major gaps; requires evidence collection | Notify owner; collect missing evidence |
| **BLOCKED_MISSING_EVIDENCE** | evidence_completeness < 30 OR critical_dependency_missing | Cannot proceed without critical evidence | Escalate; block until evidence present |

### 9.3 Risk Band Values

| Band | Completeness | Dependency Status | Auto-escalation | Human Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LOW** | ≥ 85% | No critical deps missing | None | Review optional (informational) |
| **MEDIUM** | 70–85% | 1–2 deps in progress | Notify owner | Review recommended |
| **HIGH** | 50–70% | 3+ deps missing/blocked | Escalate to coordinator | Review mandatory |
| **BLOCKED** | < 50% | Critical deps failed/missing | Escalate to leadership | Resolution required |
