## 5. Workflow Contract

### 5.1 Research Project Lifecycle
- START: `research_science` project creation with initial statuses `DRAFT/ACTIVE/ON_HOLD/COMPLETED/ARCHIVED`
- APPROVALS: human review required remains true; no autonomous approval path exists
- REVIEW: project updates route through `PATCH /api/admin/research-science/projects/{project_id}` with audit + status history in `rs_research_audit_events` and `rs_research_status_history`
- EVIDENCE: evidence attached through `POST /api/admin/research-science/evidence`; metadata-only verification
- CLOSURE: `COMPLETED` or `ARCHIVED`

### 5.2 Grant Lifecycle
- START: `research_science` grant application starts at `DRAFT`; bridge `research` grant records use `planned/active/submitted/delayed/closed`
- APPROVALS: `INTERNAL_REVIEW` and `SUBMITTED_EXTERNALLY_BY_HUMAN` prove human-reviewed progression; no autonomous grant submission
- REVIEW: patch flows through `research_science` grant updates and `research` status updates; delayed grants create delay alert records
- EVIDENCE: grant deliverables plus research evidence metadata
- CLOSURE: `CLOSED` or `ARCHIVED`

### 5.3 Publication Lifecycle
- START: `research_science` publication metadata starts at `DRAFT`; bridge `research` publication record also starts at `draft`
- APPROVALS: publication creation is fail-closed on active grant evidence; no official publication verification
- REVIEW: `SUBMITTED_FOR_REVIEW`, `EVIDENCE_PENDING`, `REVIEWED_METADATA_ONLY`, `REJECTED_METADATA_ONLY`; bridge `research` status update to `submitted` creates publication review record
- EVIDENCE: research evidence metadata plus publication review records
- CLOSURE: `REVIEWED_METADATA_ONLY`, `REJECTED_METADATA_ONLY`, or bridge `published`

### 5.4 Ethics Review Lifecycle
- START: `research_ethics` reviews default to `pending`; `research_science` ethics requests default to metadata-only request records
- APPROVALS: PI eligibility checks across faculty + faculty contracts are required before approval
- REVIEW: allowed transitions are `pending -> under_review/rejected`, `under_review -> approved/rejected/revision_requested`, `revision_requested -> under_review/rejected`
- EVIDENCE: ethics review records, amendment metadata, audit logs, and high-risk alert records
- CLOSURE: `approved`, `rejected`, or research-science `CLOSED/ARCHIVED`

### 5.5 Student Research Lifecycle
- START: `DRAFT`
- APPROVALS: supervisor assignment and milestone review remain human-reviewed
- REVIEW: statuses are `SUPERVISOR_ASSIGNED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `MILESTONE_REVIEW`, `COMPLETED_METADATA_ONLY`, `ARCHIVED`
- EVIDENCE: publication refs, conference refs, supervision refs, and evidence metadata
- CLOSURE: `COMPLETED_METADATA_ONLY` or `ARCHIVED`

### 5.6 Research KPI Lifecycle
- START: health snapshot + dashboard snapshot generation from metadata-backed sources only
- APPROVALS: no automated executive action; dashboard interpretations remain human-reviewed
- REVIEW: KPI/trend/insight/recommendation/event routes under `/api/analytics/*` and research-science dashboard/health
- EVIDENCE: dashboard snapshots, event summaries, bridge summaries, limitations, audit flags
- CLOSURE: snapshot persistence only; no autonomous enforcement path
