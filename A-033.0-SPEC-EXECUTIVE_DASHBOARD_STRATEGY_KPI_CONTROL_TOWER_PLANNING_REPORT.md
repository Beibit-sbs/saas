## 3. Strategic Context

The first Executive Governance Suite now contains two closed product verticals:
- Rector Assignment OS
- Document / Decree / Correspondence Workflow OS

The next product need is not another isolated workflow. The next layer must give leadership one consistent visibility surface above the closed workflows without fabricating metrics or bypassing evidence. That layer is the executive control tower.

Strategic role of this layer:
- summarize workflow evidence rather than replace workflows
- reveal execution health and bottlenecks at executive level
- connect strategic KPIs to real operational evidence
- remain read-only and anti-fake by design
