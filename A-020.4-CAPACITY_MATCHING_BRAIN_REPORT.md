## 7. Deterministic Scoring Model

Implemented deterministic weighted score (0–100):

- capacity fit: 30
- room type fit: 20
- computer fit: 15
- equipment fit: 15
- availability/status fit: 10
- no conflict/restriction issues: 10

Conflict penalty:

- critical: 25
- high: 15
- medium: 8
- low: 4
- capped total penalty: 40

Status mapping:

- 90–100: `excellent_match`
- 75–89: `good_match`
- 55–74: `partial_match`
- 30–54: `poor_match`
- 0–29: `not_suitable`
- explicit unavailable state: `unavailable`
- no capability case: `unknown`

Risk mapping:

- low/medium/high/critical from status + conflicts + mismatch reasons
- explicit escalation on `capacity_shortage`
- critical severity conflicts remain critical (no downgrade)
