## 6. Selection Decision

Selection decision result: PASS

Selected vertical: `Quality / Accreditation Suite`

Selection rationale:

- it is the strongest cross-vertical evidence consumer after four closed baselines
- it creates the institutional quality-assurance layer above already-implemented operational evidence
- it supports accreditation readiness, standards mapping, improvement cycles, and audit-ready documentation without requiring official submission claims
- it can remain evidence-first, metadata-only, and human-review-gated from the start
- it avoids premature provider/integration claims while still increasing strategic value for rectorate and quality leadership
